# Adani Green Energy -- Renewable Energy Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Renewable Energy Performance
Dashboard** designed to monitor and analyze key operational and
financial metrics for renewable energy generation.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of energy generation,
revenue, maintenance costs, plant performance, energy purchases, waste
generation, and maintenance activity across multiple states.

## ✨ Dashboard Highlights

-   **Total Energy Generated** -- Monitor overall energy production.
-   **Total Revenue** -- Track revenue generated from renewable energy
    operations.
-   **Maintenance Cost** -- Analyze maintenance expenditure.
-   **Total Plants** -- View the number of plants included in the
    analysis.
-   **Average Revenue per Plant** -- Compare revenue performance at
    plant level.
-   **Energy Generation by Plant** -- Breakdown of generation across
    different energy sources.
-   **Monthly Energy Generation** -- Analyze generation trends
    throughout the year.
-   **Waste Generation by Plant** -- Monitor waste distribution across
    plant types.
-   **Energy Purchase by Buyer** -- Compare energy purchases among
    buyers.
-   **Maintenance Cost by Equipment** -- Identify equipment categories
    contributing to maintenance costs.
-   **Maintenance Frequency** -- Track maintenance activity over time.
-   **State-wise Performance Summary** -- Compare revenue, plant count,
    maintenance cost, and carbon offset across states.
-   **Year Filter** -- Explore dashboard metrics for different reporting
    years.

## 🧭 Dashboard Sections

### Energy Generation

Visualizes energy generation by plant/source and provides a
month-by-month view of generation trends.

### Waste Generation

Shows the distribution of waste generation across different plant
categories.

### Energy Purchase

Compares purchased energy across buyers to support procurement analysis.

### Maintenance Analysis

Provides equipment-level maintenance cost analysis and tracks
maintenance frequency over time.

### Geographic Performance

A state-wise map and summary table provide a geographic view of plant
presence and performance.

## 🔍 Insights the Dashboard Can Support

This dashboard can be used to:

-   Monitor renewable energy generation trends.
-   Compare performance across plants and states.
-   Track revenue and maintenance expenditure.
-   Identify equipment with higher maintenance costs.
-   Analyze energy procurement patterns.
-   Monitor waste generation.
-   Review maintenance frequency over time.
-   Compare operational performance across reporting years.
